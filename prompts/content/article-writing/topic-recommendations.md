# Actionable Recommendations

After drafting a section, run this prompt to produce 2-3 practical actions your readers can take. It reinforces clarity and helps shape concluding sections or callouts.

## Prompt

```text
Based on the following content, provide 2-3 recommendations for <TARGET AUDIENCE> to act on as a detailed bullet list.

Content:
<Paste the content you have written>
```

## Example

```text
Based on the following content, provide 2-3 recommendations for small business owners to act on as a detailed bullet list.

Content:
<Paste the content you have written>
```

> [!TIP]
> In Edge's Copilot, @mention the open tab page so the prompt can reference the article draft without pasting the entire body.
