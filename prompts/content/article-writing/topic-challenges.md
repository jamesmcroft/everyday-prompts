# Audience Topic Challenges

Use this prompt while researching or outlining to identify the pain points that matter most to your audience. Refer back to the bullets as you draft sections to ensure every challenge is addressed.

## Prompt

```text
Describe 2-3 key challenges that <TARGET AUDIENCE> faces with "<TOPIC>" as detailed bullet points.
```

## Example

```text
Describe 2-3 key challenges that small business owners face with "Effective Marketing Strategies" as detailed bullet points.
```

> [!TIP]
> If your AI tool supports it, enable **Web Search** or **Agent Mode** to encourage the response to be as up-to-date and relevant as possible.
